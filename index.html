// About

I'm a junior at Syracuse University studying Applied Data Science with a concentration in data science and analytics. I transferred here from Marion Military Institute in Marion, Alabama and jumped straight into Python, SQL, data visualization, and building full-stack applications.

Most of my work lives at the overlap of data science, visualization, and AI/ML. I like taking messy datasets and turning them into something useful, whether that's a Streamlit dashboard, a visual analysis, or an automated pipeline. I care about making things that work well and look good doing it.
